Privacy policy

No first-party data collection

Effective August 7, 2026.

PM OS and the other skills published from this repository are skills-only packages. They do not connect to a server operated by Manan Suneja, create an account, use authentication, include telemetry, set cookies, or independently collect, transmit, sell, or retain personal data.

Files and prompts

The skill gives instructions to the ChatGPT, Codex, or other compatible agent that you choose. That host may read or write files only through the permissions and controls it provides. Your prompts, files, and model interactions are handled by the host product and are governed by that provider's privacy terms—not by a first-party service attached to this plugin.

Public support

Information you voluntarily include in a public GitHub issue is visible under GitHub's policies. Do not post confidential product or customer information.

Changes and contact

If a future version adds a server, account connection, telemetry, or another data flow, this policy and the plugin listing will be updated before release. Questions can be raised through the support page.
